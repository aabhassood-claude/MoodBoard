Motion principles.

Ease-out. Slow. Intentional. Motion is utility, not delight-bait. The most important thing on a screen earns the only animated moment.

Default · 120–200ms
Page · 320ms
Curve · 0.22, 1, 0.36, 1
Never · spring · overshoot
PRINCIPLE 01
Settle, don't bounce.
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
ease-out · no overshoot
PRINCIPLE 02
Staggered reveal.
40ms offset per element
orchestrated, not scattered
PRINCIPLE 03
Accent pulse on data.
500ms · once on update
earns attention, doesn't demand it
